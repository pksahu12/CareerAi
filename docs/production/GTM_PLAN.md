## 1. Market Positioning

### The White Space

Every player in reverse recruiting today is either:
- A **high-cost service business** ($2K–$15K/month) that cannot scale because it depends on human hours, or
- A **cheap automation tool** ($10–$52/month) with no intelligence, personalization, or coaching.

LaunchVector occupies the white space between them: the intelligence of a senior career strategist, the tirelessness of a 24/7 automation engine, and a data advantage that compounds with every candidate served — at 1/10th the cost of human firms.

### Positioning Statement

For mid-career professionals ($75K–$150K) who are frustrated by the cost, opacity, and inefficiency of job searching, LaunchVector is the AI-native career intelligence platform that replaces expensive human reverse recruiters with an autonomous AI agent — submitting personalized applications, reaching hiring managers, and coaching through interviews — at $149/month with no placement fees.

## 3. Target Segments

### Primary (Beachhead)

**Who:** Recently laid-off tech professionals in SF, NYC, Austin, and Seattle earning $75K–$150K.

**Why beachhead:** Highest density of candidates who:
- Understand and trust AI tools
- Have urgent, time-sensitive job search needs
- Cannot wait months for a traditional search
- Are concentrated in communities (Layoffs.fyi, Blind, LinkedIn) that enable targeted reach

**Volume:** Tech layoffs have created concentrated pools of 50K–200K active job seekers in these markets at any given time.

### Secondary

**Who:** All mid-career professionals ($75K–$150K) in tech, finance, and consulting who are actively job searching.

**Estimated addressable:** ~1–2M people in the US at any given time.

### Tertiary — B2B

**B2B Outplacement:** Companies undergoing layoffs offering LaunchVector as a severance/outplacement benefit.
- Buyer: CHRO, VP People, Head of Talent
- Price: $2K–$5K per employee for 90-day access
- Trigger: Any company with 500+ employees making layoffs

**Career Coach API:** Career coaches and boutique agencies white-labeling LaunchVector to serve their clients at scale.
- Buyer: Individual coaches with 50–500 clients
- Price: $199/seat/month
- Trigger: Coaches who see client results and want to scale their service

## 4. Phase 1 Go-To-Market (Months 1–6): Beachhead

### Channel 1 — Community-Led

**Target communities:** Layoffs.fyi community, Blind, Fishbowl, r/cscareerquestions, r/jobs, LinkedIn groups for laid-off tech workers.

**Tactic:**
- Founder posts authentic, data-driven content in these communities
- Offer 30-day free trial to recently laid-off members
- Participate in threads where candidates share frustration with the job search process
- Never pitch — add value first; let the product speak

**Expected output:** 50–200 signups/month
**Cost per user:** < $10

### Channel 2 — LinkedIn Founder Content

**Tactic:**
- Founder posts weekly data insights: "We submitted 10,000 applications last month — here's what actually gets interviews"
- Transparent, behind-the-scenes content about the product and its results
- Build audience around job search meta-insights (not generic career advice)
- Engage with comments; convert followers to trial users

**Content themes:**
- Application volume vs. response rate data
- Which ATS platforms have highest/lowest success rates
- What resume keywords correlate with interviews in specific roles
- Real candidate success stories (with permission)

**Expected output:** 100–500 followers/month; 20–80 signups/month
**Cost per user:** < $20

### Channel 3 — SEO and Content Marketing

**Target keywords:**
- "reverse recruiting service" (high intent, high cost)
- "hire someone to apply to jobs for me"
- "AI job application service"
- "automated job search"
- "best reverse recruiting alternative"
- "how much does reverse recruiting cost"

**Tactic:** 2 long-form articles per week. Publish original data stories: "LaunchVector analyzed 50,000 applications: here's what actually gets interviews." These outperform generic career advice and earn backlinks.

**Expected output:** 500–2,000 organic visits/month by Month 6
**Cost per user:** < $30

### Channel 4 — ProductHunt Launch (Month 3)

**Tactic:** Coordinated launch with beta users, career community, and founder network. Target #1 Product of the Day.

**Pre-launch checklist:**
- Recruit 50+ beta users to upvote and comment on launch day
- Line up 3–5 media mentions to publish on launch day
- Prepare demo video showing the 24-hour value delivery
- Set up referral program before launch

**Expected output:** 500–2,000 signups in 48 hours
**Cost per user:** < $5

### Channel 5 — PR and Media

**Angle:** "AI vs Human Recruiter" — the technology disruption story.

**Target outlets:** TechCrunch, Business Insider, Fast Company, Forbes, The Information.

**Story pitches:**
1. "This startup replaces $10,000 career coaches with a $149/month AI" — product launch story
2. "We analyzed 50,000 job applications. Here's what actually gets candidates hired." — data story
3. "AI is flooding hiring pipelines. Here's how candidates are fighting back." — trend story

**Expected output:** 3–5 placements; 1,000–5,000 visits each
**Cost per user:** < $15

## 5. Phase 2 Go-To-Market (Months 6–12): B2B Expansion

### Enterprise Outplacement

**Target buyers:** CHROs and VP People at tech companies with 500+ employees undergoing layoffs.

**Outreach strategy:**
- Identify layoff events via Layoffs.fyi, LinkedIn news, and tech press
- Reach CHRO via warm intro through investor/advisor network where possible
- Cold email sequence referencing the specific layoff event + LaunchVector outcome data
- Offer a pilot: 20 employees, 30-day access, measure placement rate

**Deal structure:** $2K–$5K per employee for 90 days of access
**Target:** 2 signed enterprise deals by Month 10

**Validation question for CHRO interviews:** "Would you pay $2K per employee to guarantee they land a job within 60 days?" — target 30%+ saying yes with budget authority.

### Career Coach API Partnership

**Target:** Career coaches with 100–500 follower audiences on LinkedIn or YouTube who currently serve clients manually.

**Acquisition strategy:**
1. Offer 10 coaches free API access for 60 days
2. Track adoption, client results, and willingness to pay $199/seat/month
3. Coaches who see results become advocates — one coach converting = 50–200 future candidate leads
4. Build a partner directory of certified LaunchVector coaches

**Deal structure:** $199/seat/month (unlimited client applications)

### Staffing Agency White-Label

**Target:** Boutique staffing agencies that want a candidate-side service line without the operational overhead.

**Pitch:** White-label LaunchVector under their brand to differentiate their service offering and add a recurring software revenue stream.

## 6. Viral Growth Loops

| Loop | Mechanism | Why It Compounds |
|---|---|---|
| **Placement Loop** | User lands job → LaunchVector sends congratulations + requests LinkedIn testimonial → User posts "I got 12 interviews in 3 weeks" → Peers in job search see and sign up | Job placements are high-emotion moments with strong sharing intent. Peer networks have similar profiles = high conversion. |
| **Data Network Effect** | More candidates → more application outcome data → ML models predict better → applications perform better → more interviews → more word-of-mouth → more candidates | Each new user makes the product better for all users. Competitors cannot replicate this without equivalent data scale. |
| **Coach Flywheel** | Coach uses LaunchVector → client gets results → coach publicly attributes results to LaunchVector → other coaches see and inquire | Coaches have built-in audiences and trust. One converted coach = 50–200 future candidate leads. |
| **Enterprise Testimonial Loop** | Company uses LaunchVector for outplacement → employees get jobs faster → CHRO shares story at HR conferences → other CHROs evaluate LaunchVector | Enterprise logos create B2B credibility that unlocks more enterprise deals. Self-reinforcing. |

## 7. Referral Program

- **Trigger:** Activate at the moment of job placement — highest emotional moment, strongest sharing intent
- **Referrer reward:** $50 credit toward next month
- **Referee reward:** 1 month free (on top of standard trial)
- **Mechanism:** In-app "Share your win" flow; pre-written LinkedIn post template
- **Target K-factor:** 0.3–0.5 at maturity
