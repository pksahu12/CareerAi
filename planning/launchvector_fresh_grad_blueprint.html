The Problem

Fresh graduates face a brutal asymmetry: 200+ applicants per entry-level role, ATS systems that reject unoptimised resumes automatically, and zero budget for $2K–$15K human reverse recruiters. The $40K–$60K salary cohort is the largest group of active job seekers and the least served by any premium tool.

The Solution

A graduate-specific AI tier that delivers the core magic of LaunchVector — resume tailoring, autonomous ATS applications, hiring manager outreach, and interview coaching — tuned for entry-level roles, internship pipelines, and campus hiring cycles.

Why Now

Structural

AI flooding pipelines means 200+ applicants per entry role. Graduates without optimised applications are invisible.

Market timing

2025–2026 layoff cycle has created record graduate unemployment — demand for job search tools is at an all-time high.

Tech maturity

Browser automation, LLM fine-tuning, and voice AI are now reliable enough to replace 80%+ of manual application labor.

No winner

No reverse recruiting firm serves graduates — they can't afford the price. No SaaS tool serves them with intelligence — only volume.

Customer personas — graduates

Persona A — "The Overwhelmed STEM Grad"

CS grad, 22, applying to 80+ jobs manually with 1 callback. Spends 15 hrs/week on job boards. Knows something is wrong. Would pay $29/mo without hesitation if it promises more callbacks.

Persona B — "The Ambitious Arts Grad"

English grad, 22, trying to break into marketing or UX. Doesn't know how to make resume "ATS-proof." Needs coaching on how to frame non-technical skills for tech-adjacent roles.

Persona C — "The International Student"

Engineering grad on OPT/Tier 2 visa. Applies only to roles that sponsor. Huge pain filtering 500 jobs by sponsorship status. High willingness to pay — stakes are extremely high.

Persona D — "The University Careers Team"

B2B angle: university careers services offices. Buy institutional access for all students. One deal = 5,000+ grad users. Budget = £20K–£100K/year. Replaces generic career portals.

Competitive advantages

Entry-level ML model
Training outcome data on graduate roles specifically — which entry-level JDs convert, which ATS platforms screen grads out. No competitor has this data at the graduate level.
Campus hiring intelligence
Grad scheme cycles, early application deadlines, and internship pipelines are predictable. LaunchVector Grad surfaces them before competitors know they exist.
University B2B flywheel
One institutional deal unlocks hundreds of warm users. Careers offices become the distribution channel — replacing expensive paid acquisition.
Upsell to Professional
Graduates who land entry-level roles will use LaunchVector again in 2–3 years for their first career move. CAC paid once, LTV multiplied across career.

Risks & mitigation

HIGH Thin resumes
AI has less material to work with — mitigate by asking richer onboarding questions (projects, societies, GPA, internships) and training prompts on graduate-format resumes specifically.
HIGH Low willingness to pay
Graduates have low disposable income. Counter: $29 is less than Netflix + Spotify combined. Offer student email discount (verified via .edu / .ac.uk). Trial converts at higher rate when urgency is high.
MED ATS platform blocking
Same risk as Pro tier. Multi-method submission (API-first, fingerprint randomisation, human fallback for high-value platforms like Goldman Sachs's custom ATS).
LOW Brand perception
Graduates may feel "AI job searching" feels inauthentic. Counter with transparency: full dashboard showing every action taken, candidate controls what goes out. Position as "your AI career co-pilot, not a bot."

Viral growth loops — graduate-specific

1. First job testimonial flywheel

Landing your first job is one of the highest-emotion life moments — higher than any career milestone after. Graduates post extensively on LinkedIn ("Excited to announce I'm joining..."). LaunchVector prompts a testimonial + referral code at that moment. Every testimonial reaches 200–500 peers in job search mode.

2. University cohort network effect

When one student in a cohort gets more callbacks, they tell their coursemates. University social networks are tight and high-trust. One conversion in a cohort of 200 students → 10–20 organic sign-ups within 2 weeks. No paid acquisition needed.

3. Careers office institutional loop

One careers office buys institutional access → placement rate improves → careers office presents at Times Higher Education / NACAC conference → 10+ other universities evaluate LaunchVector. Enterprise logo creates credibility that unlocks more enterprise deals.

4. Long-term upsell compounding

Every graduate placed becomes a Professional tier subscriber in 2–5 years when they want their second job. CAC paid once at $29/mo acquisition price. LTV captured at $149/mo Professional pricing. CLV across career could exceed $2,000 per graduate placed.

Execution Roadmap

30-day roadmap — Foundation

Week 1 — Legal & infrastructure

Delaware C-Corp incorporated. Co-founder equity + IP assignment executed. Domain acquired. Stripe, Supabase, Vercel configured. Landing page live with graduate waitlist capture.

Week 2 — Graduate discovery

15 graduate job-seeker interviews (target: recently graduated CS, engineering, business students). Document pain points. Rank features by urgency. Identify 3 university career society leads for partnership conversations.

Week 3 — MVP scoping

Define thin MVP: resume parsing → job matching → 5 ATS platform submissions → dashboard. Wireframe onboarding flow tuned for graduate CVs (projects, GPA, societies). Engineering spec written for Greenhouse + Lever (most common grad-employer ATS).

Week 4 — Build begins + university outreach

CTO begins Playwright-based ATS handler. Full-stack engineer wireframes dashboard. First university careers office contacted with free pilot offer. 25 waitlist sign-ups from founder's graduate network.

90-day roadmap — Beta to launch

Weeks 5–6 — Private beta (50 graduates)

Recruit beta users from university career societies + personal network. Daily feedback calls. Track interview rate obsessively. Target: >15% of applications generate recruiter response.

Week 7 — Quality hardening

Reach >90% ATS submit success rate across 5 platforms. Fix edge cases in graduate CV parsing (projects, dissertations, extracurriculars). Improve onboarding UX for 22-year-olds unfamiliar with job search tools.

Week 8 — Monetisation setup

Stripe billing live at $29/month Grad tier. Student email verification (.edu / .ac.uk) for 10% discount. Trial-to-paid conversion flow. Cancellation flow with feedback capture ("Did you find a job? Tell us about it.").

Weeks 9–10 — ProductHunt + university launch

Coordinated ProductHunt launch with beta community. First university careers office pilot goes live (free for 200 students, 90 days). Founder posts data story on TikTok + LinkedIn.

Weeks 11–12 — First 100 paying students

Activate LinkedIn/TikTok content strategy. SEO articles: "How to get a graduate job in 2026 with AI." Launch referral code system. Graduate testimonials begin accumulating.
